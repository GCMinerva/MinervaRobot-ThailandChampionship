COORDINATE SYSTEM:
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

                            Robot Forward (0°)
                                    |
                                    |
              Left Side (-90°) <─────+────→ Right Side (+90°)
                        (Q2)              (Q1)
                                    |
                                  Goal

  • 0°   = Robot's forward direction
  • +90° = Right side (Q1 relative to robot)
  • -90° = Left side (Q2 relative to robot)
  • Range: ±90° from robot heading
